On 7 August 2020 Ofqual described the planned use of school estimates and an ordering of pupils. Awarding bodies would moderate the submissions to make standards comparable across centres.
Ofqual said school judgments would carry greater weight for subjects or centres with relatively few entries. It intended to publish further details the following week.
Ofqual said a centre with evidence of an error or unreliable moderation would be able to appeal.
