In its retrospective study of A levels in England, the GRADE research team recorded that exam boards issued calculated grades on 13 August 2020.
The team reported that on 17 August Ofqual told awarding bodies to reissue A level grades using the better of the teacher estimate and the calculated result.
Across A level entries, teachers' assessments exceeded the calculated result in 39 percent of cases, according to the GRADE researchers.
Across individual candidates, 68 percent received an upgrade in at least one A level subject when final grades were issued.
The researchers could identify people with the largest discrepancies across subjects. Their data could not establish whose distress or other consequences were greatest.
According to the study, the calculation used the school's ranking of students, its past subject results and information about the prior attainment of its current pupils.
The researchers could not determine whether a school estimate or calculated result would have come closer to an individual's performance had the cancelled summer examinations taken place.
