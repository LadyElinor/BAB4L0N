The Department for Education announced on 17 December 2020 that more than 10,000 people would receive AS and A level results following examinations held in October.
The department reported that around 16 percent of autumn A level entries came from students with no summer grade in the same subject.
Where a summer result was available for comparison, the department reported an improvement for almost half of the students at A level and just under 40 percent at AS.
The department cautioned against comparing grade boundaries across exam series because the autumn awards represented such a small proportion of normal summer volume.
