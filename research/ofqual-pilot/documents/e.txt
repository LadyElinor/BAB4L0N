On 25 May 2021 Ofqual explained that the autumn examinations had offered private candidates who could not obtain a summer grade another opportunity to earn one.
The autumn sitting also gave people disappointed by their summer grade a chance to improve it. Ofqual distinguished this exceptional series from the usual November GCSE sitting.
Ofqual counted 8,885 candidates who took autumn A level examinations in England, across 1,795 centres.
Ofqual recorded 13,275 candidates taking the autumn GCSE examinations. The November series was restricted to English language and mathematics.
