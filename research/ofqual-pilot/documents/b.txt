On 17 August 2020 Ofqual chair Roger Taylor announced that the summer award in England would be the better of a school or college estimate and the moderated result. The change covered AS and A levels and GCSEs.
Taylor acknowledged students' anxiety and distress following the results. In his account, relying on schools to lodge appeals added to teachers' workload and prolonged uncertainty for young people.
Taylor said the Secretary of State had asked Ofqual to devise a way to maintain qualification standards and keep overall results broadly comparable with earlier years.
Taylor said discussions with universities and the Department for Education were already under way.
