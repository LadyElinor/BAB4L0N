In guidance dated 26 August 2020, Ofqual reported that students had received whichever was better, the centre assessment grade or the standardised grade.
Ofqual explained that most students concerned about how their school or college handled their grade should first discuss the matter there. An unresolved complaint could then go to the exam board.
Ofqual identified a separate route for a student with evidence of bias or discrimination who did not wish to take it to their own institution. They could give the evidence directly to the relevant exam board.
According to Ofqual, a finding of malpractice would lead the exam board to consider changing the student's grade.
Ofqual noted that schools made the estimates for the summer award at a different time and for a different purpose than the grades predicted for university applications.
