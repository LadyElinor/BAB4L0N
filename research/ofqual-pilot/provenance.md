# England's grading reversal in 2020

This single, purposively selected case concerns a statistical standardisation process in education and the arrangements made after its August reversal. It broadens an AI-oversight retrieval exercise to automated public decision-making. It is historical, geographically limited to England and does not involve a large language model. All passages are short curator-written factual paraphrases. They carry no canonical status as official transcripts. Retrieve against documents/ only.
