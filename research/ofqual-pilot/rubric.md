## Q1 · Initial results and later award

The 13 August A level awards were calculated grades (f.txt#L1). On 26 August, Ofqual reported actual receipt of the higher of the centre's assessment and the standardised result (c.txt#L1). For a question about the rule in effect, accept either the chair's 17 August announcement (b.txt#L1) or the subsequent account of the direction to reissue grades (f.txt#L2). Reserve any claim of completed receipt for the guidance or equivalent evidence of receipt. The 7 August plan alone cannot establish what was issued. Preserve the distinction between an ordered remedy and delivery if only the former was retrieved.

## Q2 · Access to redress

Ofqual's 26 August guidance makes direct submission to the awarding body available to someone with evidence of bias or discrimination who chooses to bypass the school (c.txt#L3). If malpractice is established the board considers a grade change (c.txt#L4). Avoid promising an automatic correction or suggesting the school invariably must hear the complaint first. Merely retrieving the ordinary sequence (c.txt#L2) cannot resolve the exception. No individual complaint outcome is included.

## Q3 · Denominators

Of the subject entries analysed, 39 percent had a higher teacher estimate than their calculated result (f.txt#L3). Of the people analysed, 68 percent gained in at least one subject (f.txt#L4). A person can contribute multiple entries. Rounded percentages with the correct denominators and direction are sufficient. The separate percentages for students sitting autumn examinations describe a different population and time.

## Q4 · An offered and used route

The autumn examination series gave private candidates who could not receive a summer award an opportunity to take a qualification (e.txt#L1). DfE reported that about 16 percent of autumn A level entries had no summer grade in that subject (d.txt#L2). As stated, the second part asks about students without a summer grade. Their private-candidate status was not established by the percentage. Either the 8,885 candidates (e.txt#L3) or DfE's total across AS and A levels (d.txt#L1) can support that an examination took place but neither isolates those without a summer grade. Credit a candid partial answer. Do not infer universal access, uptake or redress.

## Q5 · Reported distress and limits

Ofqual's chair acknowledged anxiety and distress (b.txt#L2). The GRADE researchers recorded that a large grade discrepancy could be measured but did not identify who suffered most (f.txt#L5). An official's acknowledgment is useful attributed evidence; it is not testimony from a pupil. A measured difference in grades cannot substitute for an observed measure of harm to a particular person. If only one part is evidenced, qualify the other.

Warranted abstention for a number. The bounded collection contains no linked records of initial lost offers, subsequent readmission to the same choice and the original deadline.
