## Scope

The UK passages describe the 2017 circulation transition and the design competition that preceded it. Returned-coin totals and the Mint's stated reuse plans address different stages of the process. The Bennu passages cover site selection, collection and the early accounting for returned material. The figures reported for the total and the Canadian allocation do not resolve an individual stone's mass. The census passages cover national counts and rules, with Texas as a state example. The Texas history gives legislative milestones and leaves the bill's authorship unspecified.

The selected passages constitute the reading corpus. The linked records contain additional material. Two sources on the same website may describe the same underlying event, and the space agencies and university were mission partners. The publisher IDs identify issuers and do not represent independent measurement.
