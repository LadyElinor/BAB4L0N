The Canadian Space Agency reported that the Bennu capsule reached the Utah desert on September 24, 2023.
The landing bulletin credits Canada's OSIRIS-REx Laser Altimeter with surveying the shape of the asteroid to help compare possible sampling locations.
The Canadian agency named NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston as the destination for curation and distribution of the material.
Canadian researchers and staff from the space agency were to help choose the pieces for the country's collection.
