The Census Bureau counted 331,449,281 people living in the 50 states and the District of Columbia for the national resident total on April 1, 2020.
Texas added 3,999,944 residents over the decade from 2010 to 2020, the largest numerical increase of any state in the bureau's release.
Utah led the percentage comparison, with resident growth of 18.4 percent over the decade.
The bureau put Puerto Rico's own 2020 resident total at 3,285,874 after a decline of 11.8 percent.
Commerce Secretary Gina Raimondo delivered the apportionment counts to President Joe Biden on April 26, 2021, according to the Census Bureau.
