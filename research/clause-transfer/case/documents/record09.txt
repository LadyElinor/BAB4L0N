The University of Arizona's before-and-after pictures mark Nightingale as the place where OSIRIS-REx touched Bennu to collect its sample.
The university reports that the spacecraft passed within 2.3 miles of Bennu on its April 7, 2021 return to photograph the area.
The imaging team timed the pictures for close to local noon to reduce shadows in the comparison.
The team described a possible depression at the contact site with several large boulders visible at the bottom.
