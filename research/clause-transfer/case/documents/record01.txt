The Royal Mint dates the twelve-sided pound's entry into use as legal tender to 28 March 2017.
In the Mint's account of the original reverse, the rose, thistle, leek and shamrock shared a stem inside a coronet.
The Mint credits Jody Clark with the Queen Elizabeth II portrait on the heads side of the 2017 pound.
The Mint records the withdrawal of the round pound's legal tender status at 23:59 on 15 October 2017.
The replacement weighed 8.75 grams and had a thickness of 2.8 millimetres, according to the Mint.
