HM Treasury named David Pearce, a 15-year-old pupil in Walsall, as winner of the competition for the reverse of the new pound coin.
The Royal Mint ran the competition for the Treasury and received more than 6,000 entries.
The Treasury said the drawing used a shamrock, a rose, a thistle and a leek growing out of a royal coronet to represent the UK.
Coin artist David Lawrence and lettering specialist Stephen Raw helped prepare the drawing for minting.
The Treasury said some other entrants had proposed seaside piers or cups of tea to represent Britain.
