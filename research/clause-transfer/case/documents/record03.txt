A joint Treasury and Royal Mint announcement dated 10 July 2017 put production of the twelve-sided pound at one billion coins.
The announcement said 800 million of the old round pounds had already been returned.
Exchequer Secretary Andrew Jones visited the Royal Mint's Welsh headquarters and struck the coin that marked the occasion.
The Mint's chief executive expected to reuse metal from many returned round pounds by melting them down to strike the new design.
The Treasury expected the replacement to outnumber the old coins in circulation by the end of July.
