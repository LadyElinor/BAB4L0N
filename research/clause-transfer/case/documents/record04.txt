The Canadian Space Agency says its OSIRIS-REx Laser Altimeter mapped Bennu's surface shape in three dimensions to guide the choice of a collection site.
Four areas were considered before the team settled on Nightingale, where the Canadian agency says OSIRIS-REx collected material on October 20, 2020.
Canada's agreed share in return for supplying the instrument amounted to four percent of the recovered sample, according to the space agency.
The agency attributes its global model of Bennu's relief to roughly twenty million measurements.
The Canadian Space Agency credits MDA with building the instrument and Optech with a substantial contribution.
