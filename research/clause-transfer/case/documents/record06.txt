The Texas Legislative Council records a rise from 36 U.S. House districts for Texas after the 2010 census to 38 after the 2020 census.
Texas legislators passed the 2021 congressional map in Senate Bill 6 on October 19, during the third called session.
The governor signed the state's congressional redistricting bill on October 25, 2021.
Texas House Bill 1, passed on October 16 that year, dealt with boundaries for the state House.
