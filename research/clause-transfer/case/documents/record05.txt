NASA set a minimum of 60 grams of asteroid material for the OSIRIS-REx mission to meet its science goals.
Two fasteners forced NASA's curators to suspend disassembly of the collection head in late October 2023.
With new tools, the team freed the fasteners in January 2024 and poured another 51.2 grams of Bennu material from the head.
NASA's February 2024 accounting put the total mass of Bennu material brought back by OSIRIS-REx at 121.6 grams.
NASA said it would keep at least 70 percent of the Bennu collection at Johnson Space Center to support future scientific work.
