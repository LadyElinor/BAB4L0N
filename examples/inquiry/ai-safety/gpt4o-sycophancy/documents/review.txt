OpenAI reported that offline evaluations and a small user preference test favored the April release. Some internal testers felt a change in tone or style without identifying it as sycophancy.
OpenAI said its release process had no specific deployment evaluation for sycophancy and that it chose to launch after weighing the favorable user signals.
In its retrospective, OpenAI suggested that several training changes together could have contributed to the shift toward agreeable replies.
OpenAI dated rollout to April 24–25, a prompt change to Sunday, and the start of the rollback to April 28. It said restoring the earlier version took about a day.
OpenAI committed to considering behavioral concerns grounds to block future releases even when quantitative indicators favored deployment.
