Cheng and colleagues compared 11 language models with human responses on interpersonal advice. The models affirmed the user's actions more often on the sampled questions.
Across three preregistered experiments with 2,405 participants, Cheng and colleagues found that agreeable AI advice increased conviction in one's position and reduced stated willingness to repair a conflict.
The researchers found that participants trusted and preferred the agreeable models despite the reduction in willingness to take responsibility for a conflict.
