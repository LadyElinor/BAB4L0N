# West Calumet household notice

**Finding.** The reviewed public records support a prevention campaign by July 6, 2016. Later evidence supports the start of a property-results mailing in early June. In these ten sources we did not identify an acknowledgment or resident account establishing receipt of a particular home's 2014–15 yard-soil results by July 6. The number of households that had received those results by the deadline remains unknown.

## Evidence chronology

| Event | What can be established from the selected records |
| --- | --- |
| November 2014 | EPA announced further sampling and prospective meetings with property owners. The fact sheet records a plan. A tenant and a property owner may be different recipients. [EPA fact sheet, p. 1](https://semspub.epa.gov/work/05/919976.pdf) |
| May 2016 | A city timeline and an EPA declaration put transfer of property-level information to the city on May 24. A later court order gives May 26 for provision of results to the city and housing authority. Their wording differs, so the dates could concern different transfers. All are retrospective, and each names institutional recipients. [City timeline, p. 1](https://bloximages.chicago2.vip.townnews.com/content/tncms/assets/v3/editorial/9/98/998c8b21-ccee-5480-889b-400165345ace/57cb53916cd55.pdf.pdf) · [EPA declaration, ¶31](https://semspub.epa.gov/work/05/937049.pdf) · [Rolan, document 169](https://docs.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/indiana/inndce/1:2016cv00357/88023/169/) |
| Early June | The court later described the start of EPA's results mailing to residents. That establishes evidence of dispatch. It gives no receipt date or count for the cutoff. [Rolan, document 152, pp. 3–4](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/indiana/inndce/1:2016cv00357/88023/152/) |
| June 29 and July 5–6 | The city later reported a June canvass. Janet Pope later identified July 5 prevention flyers. EPA's July 6 public release describes door-to-door exposure advice. A household could receive useful safety information while still waiting for its yard measurements. [City timeline, p. 2](https://bloximages.chicago2.vip.townnews.com/content/tncms/assets/v3/editorial/9/98/998c8b21-ccee-5480-889b-400165345ace/57cb53916cd55.pdf.pdf) · [Pope, ¶58](https://semspub.epa.gov/work/05/937051.pdf) · [July 6 release](https://www.epa.gov/archive/epa/newsreleases/epa-takes-steps-reduce-lead-exposure-west-calumet-housing-complex-east-chicago-ind.html) |
| July 8 and after | The court identifies a July 8 results letter to Rolan. Pope records copies of mailed results letters going to city, school and park officials on July 13. Neither record establishes household receipt by July 6. The city's timeline describes a July 11–14 mailing under a July 11, **2015** heading placed among July 2016 events. We retained the printed year and left its relationship to the June mailing unresolved. [Rolan, document 152](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/indiana/inndce/1:2016cv00357/88023/152/) · [Pope, ¶60](https://semspub.epa.gov/work/05/937051.pdf) · [City timeline, p. 2](https://bloximages.chicago2.vip.townnews.com/content/tncms/assets/v3/editorial/9/98/998c8b21-ccee-5480-889b-400165345ace/57cb53916cd55.pdf.pdf) |
| Resident accounts | A former resident's public comment specifically recalls receiving the mayor's relocation letter and the difficulty of finding replacement housing. A broadcast interview describes the urgent question of where the family could go. Neither passage dates receipt of the household's laboratory results. These distinct publications need not represent independent households. [Public comment dated January 14, 2019](https://semspub.epa.gov/work/05/946378.pdf) · [Published interview](https://www.wlrn.org/2017-08-07/questions-linger-for-public-housing-residents-who-lived-at-a-superfund-site) |

The [supplied Zone 1 page](https://www.epa.gov/uss-lead-superfund-site/ou1-zone-1-west-calumet-housing-complex-east-chicago-ind) says that all residential yards were sampled and links de-identified measurements. Its current version entered only the expanded pass. Sampling coverage alone cannot supply a notice denominator, a household-specific delivery history or evidence of understanding. We used the landing page's description and did not import addresses or individual measurements.

## Reader comparison

Run `python scripts/west_calumet_trial.py` from the repository root. The [protocol and scoring rule](PROTOCOL.md) and [passage register](case.json) were [published before running either reader](https://github.com/LadyElinor/BAB4L0N/commit/67a6894e5c7fe23101c39cb7922b290a21030d2c). Both readers saw the same editorial paraphrases and had four credits per pass. The first pass uses two contemporary publications and five passages. The expanded pass uses ten publications and eighteen passages. The [machine-readable returns](../../../reports/west-calumet-notice-v1.json) preserve every retrieved reference, including repetitions.

| Pass | Babalon target rows | Keyword target rows | Inspections used, Babalon / keyword |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| By-cutoff publications | 1 / 2 | 1 / 2 | 2 / 4 |
| With later publications | 2 / 4 | 1 / 4 | 4 / 4 |

Both first-pass readers retrieved general outreach and missed the owner-meeting plan. In the expanded pass both recovered the June dispatch passage. Babalon also recovered the resident comment, spending two inspections on the same city passage across roles. Keyword search uniquely recovered the city's ambiguous mailing entry and the court's institutional-transfer date. These relevant passages deserve attention even though they earned no target credit. Both missed the specifically dated July 8 individual letter and Pope's July 5 entry. No source in either pack cleared the receipt threshold. The historical assessment above uses manual review of the wider packet. The programs were scored on retrieval only.

**What could change this finding:** a privacy-protected delivery acknowledgment tied to an individual results letter and dated by July 6, or existing recipient testimony identifying the content and when it arrived. A batch mailing register could clarify dispatch timing and reconcile the city timeline without establishing personal receipt. An already-published record of difficulty interpreting the figures would address a further, separate question about whether notice could be acted upon. This exercise involved no resident contact, clinical assessment or determination of liability.
