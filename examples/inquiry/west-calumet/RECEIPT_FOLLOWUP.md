# Household receipt follow-up

**Question.** Does an already-public account place delivery of a particular Zone 1 household's 2014–15 yard-soil results on or before July 6, 2016?

**Finding.** The plaintiffs' account narrows the timing of household-specific information to July. The sources examined do not place receipt on either side of July 6 for any household with adequate precision. The number that had received their own results by that date remains unknown. The earlier court-summarized evidence of June dispatch still stands.

## Remaining documentary lead

The public EPA FOIA log identifies a September 7, 2016 request, EPA-R5-2016-010001, concerning project correspondence and West Calumet notices. The log is a locator. No release package containing a household acknowledgment was located in this search. A redacted transmittal paired to a dated acknowledgment of the same yard result, or an already-published resident statement placing that result before the cutoff, would change the finding for that household. A batch mailing log could settle dispatch questions on its own. [EPA request log, search the request number](https://19january2021snapshot.epa.gov/sites/static/files/2017-07/documents/all_pending_report_20170705-sorted.pdf)

The search covered public court orders, declarations and exhibits, EPA outreach histories and FOIA indexing, and contemporaneous and retrospective press accounts. It was not a review of a complete discovery production or a complete agency mail register. No resident was contacted and no records request was sent. This is a manual documentary follow-up.
