# Source attribution follow-up, November 12, 2026

On the original 32 exposed handoffs, changing the answerer's citation discipline
corrected both known errors when a timing receipt was available. Without a receipt
the revised answerer abstained on those cases. On independent financial-report
question wording the revision changed zero source predictions.

| Answerer | Purchase treatment | Charged inspections | Correct handoffs / 32 | Supported answers | Wrong-source answers |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen | No purchase | 0 | 20 | 12 | 2 |
| Source-bound | No purchase | 0 | 20 | 12 | 0 |
| Frozen | Request once | 28 | 30 | 21 | 2 |
| Source-bound | Request once | 28 | 32 | 23 | 0 |

Both answerers saw the same actual receipt in each matched treatment. Four
requests in the one-purchase condition were outside the grant and cost zero.
At fixed exposure, the two previously unsupported answers became supported
answers on cases 7 and 27. Both used the same known template and both had a
reported count equal to the event count. The new procedure combines a
request-clause interpretation rule with explicit source slots. The study
does not isolate the effect of each component or establish that an adaptive
policy achieves 32/32 with the original selective arm's ten-credit expenditure.

In the separate reviewer-wording contrast, each question appeared with a
summary count of two and an event count of either two or four. With the
receipt, the original answered 2/4 correctly and the source-bound version
answered 4/4. With no purchase both completed 2/4; the revised answerer
abstained when asked for the event count. The frozen grader also checked the open objection and
fresh-observation obligation.

## Independently authored questions

On a public pack containing 16 TAT-QA prose-source and 16 table-source
questions, **both policies scored 15/32**. Both identified 10/16 prose
answers and 5/16 table answers. Both made five unmapped predictions.
Always choosing either source scores 16/32 on this balanced pack. No question
received a different source prediction under the revised policy.

The external authors provided the question text and source annotations. Our
cross-domain mapping, sampling and evaluation are study-author decisions.
Only wording reached the worker; TAT-QA labels locate answers in actual
financial report passages and tables. This is a source-routing diagnostic.
It supplies no evidence of a portable improvement, full financial QA or
independent custody of a Commonwealth handoff trial. No promotion occurred.
