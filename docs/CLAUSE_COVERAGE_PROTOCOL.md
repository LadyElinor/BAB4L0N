# Prospective clause-coverage comparison

**Decision.** Test whether greedily covering both parts of a question helps a reader deliver a complete evidence set in four inspections. The primary comparison is clause coverage versus reciprocal-rank fusion (RRF) with four candidates per component list. Fixed BM25 and the frozen exclusive-slot rule are additional controls. Eight candidates per list is the sole sensitivity condition. All readers share each condition's input rankings and eligible pool. The public Ofqual rankings and their depth sensitivity were known to the developer. Ofqual is development only.

## Readers and costs

Use the existing tokenizer, BM25 parameters, and clause reader. Split at a single occurrence of “and”, “but” or “versus” when each side has at least two distinct content words and the full ranking has at least two results. The candidate pool is the union of the original full-query and composite clause-BM25 returns at the stated depth. The candidate may use the two constituent clause rank positions as metadata. A passage appearing only in a constituent ranking stays outside the pool.

The new selector adapts explicit query-aspect diversification to required clauses. For each candidate passage let whole-query and composite-list relevance be their RRF score scaled by 61/2, so a passage ranked first in both has relevance one. Use rank origin one, RRF constant 60 and zero for absence. For each clause, use reciprocal rank as a bounded match strength. Initially each clause has residual weight one. At each step average the two residual-weighted match strengths, combine that novelty term and relevance with equal weight, and select the maximum. Break ties by relevance, then lexical passage ID. After selection multiply each residual by one minus that passage's clause match strength. When splitting is ineligible, fall back to RRF. No parameters are fitted. [xQuAD paper](https://terrierteam.dcs.gla.ac.uk/publications/ecir2010_rodrygo_div.pdf)

Use the full-query top four for the fixed reader. Use the PR #21 implementations for RRF and the exclusive rule. Deliver at most four unique passages per question. Record all native returns. Re-select all arms at the smallest attained spend across both depths if necessary. Keep zeros. Raise a visible run failure for duplicate or unmatched returns. The selector operates on rank IDs. The evidence worksheet materializes only selected bodies in lexical ID order. Within each condition compute each distinct BM25 query once. Log standalone BM25 calls, scored query–passage pairs and input ID positions per arm, including the constituent lists for the coverage reader. Time standalone ranking plus selection once warm and five measured repetitions per method/depth on the fresh case packet. Report median total latency for the packet on one host separately from the deterministic receipt. Use the same in-memory corpus and single process, rotate arm order across repetitions, and include building the tokenized index inside BM25.

## Blinding and decision rule

Randomize opaque aliases across all eight method/depth combinations independently per question. Use identical neutral forms for all returns. Within a question, identical evidence sets may share a single interpretation and review, expanded to every alias before sealing. Retain the worksheet and private-key digests outside the run before interpretation. Seal the interpreter's cited answers before two separate blinded reviewers receive them. Give reviewers the questions, actual returns, answers and scoring instructions. Keep targets and identities private. Record each review digest independently and close the predeclared roster before opening the key. Use the maintained v2 custody and v3 aligned-decision and tally code. Preserve the original texts, citations, disagreements, unsupported claims, uncertainty and warranted or unwarranted abstentions for each reviewer.

Report results by condition, case and reviewer. For the primary pair show the leave-one-case-out range of the complete-coverage rate difference. A lead worth testing externally needs more complete sets than RRF and fixed BM25 at the primary depth, gains in at least two cases, a positive net difference against both at the sensitivity depth, and no increase in unsupported claims or unwarranted abstentions against either control for either reviewer. These are descriptive advancement criteria for a larger study. All outcomes here remain `NOT_PROMOTED`. Independent human custody and blinded human review of a larger unseen multi-case sample are required for a transfer or answer-quality claim.
