# Independently selected AI safety questions: review protocol v1

This is a protocol and an offline runner, not a completed independent evaluation. No promotion is possible here. The two Relay Atlas packets and previously scored questions are exposed development material. Do not present a permutation of them as a holdout.

**Before either reader runs:** a custodian outside implementation selects previously unopened questions and a bounded collection of original or attributed documents. Include changes implemented versus safeguards promised, contrary evidence, missing affected-user observations, and questions the collection cannot resolve. State selection and exclusions, institutional source families (syndication does not create independent witnesses), the interpreter, two separate reviewers, timing, and any prior exposure. A name or SHA does not prove independence; preserve the custody record separately. Do not change code, questions, grants or rubric after publishing their commitment. Failure or withdrawal stays in the record.

Put UTF-8 documents in one directory. Give the custodian a JSON packet like this (illustrative, not a study):
```json
{"budget":4,"families":{"minutes.txt":"minutes-origin","account.txt":"participant"},"questions":[{"id":"q1","question":"Which change was completed and which was only proposed?"}]}
```
Write a private rubric before inspection. For every question specify the decision or time boundary, acceptable equivalent passages and their source families, unsupported inferences, and when abstention is warranted. Include a way to flag an overlooked account or challenge the rubric. Keep grades and targets out of the packet and the documents. The reader need not have a label for everything valuable.

1. Run `python scripts/blind_inquiry.py freeze packet.json documents RUBRIC_SHA256 freeze.json`. RUBRIC_SHA256 is the SHA-256 of the exact private rubric file. Deposit the printed commitment with an independent custodian **before** running. The freeze binds the protocol, implemented methods, source bytes, family register, questions, predeclared grant and rubric hash.
2. Run `python scripts/blind_inquiry.py run packet.json documents freeze.json PUBLISHED_HASH new-private-directory`. Keep the printed worksheet and private-key digests separately. The key has arm identities, native-spend traces and matched-spend traces. Share only the worksheet with the interpreter. Labels are randomized separately per question; the same returned passages can still suggest a method, so blinding is imperfect. No model or external action is used.
3. The common interpreter records one answer or abstention for each anonymous result. Use the structure below; provide an interpretation, the relevant uncertainty and an unanswered question separately. Answering requires a citation in the returned set. Run `python scripts/blind_inquiry.py seal-answers worksheet.json answers.json`, deposit its digest, and give the unchanged answers and worksheet to the two reviewers. The custodian may now give them the precommitted rubric, but **not the method key or each other's decisions**.
4. Reviewers assess each answer against the record and rubric, crediting equivalent evidence. They record separate judgments, citations, reasons and unanswered questions; there is no required majority conclusion. Use one review file per person. A question without an informative matched comparison stays visible. Only after both reviews are frozen, run `python scripts/blind_inquiry.py collect worksheet.json custodian-only.json KEY_HASH answers.json rubric.txt freeze.json PUBLISHED_HASH result.json review1.json review2.json`.
5. Share the term-by-term disagreements, complete natural and matched spending, unsupported claims, abstentions, exclusions and unclassified concerns. Do not interpret a retrieval hit as an effect on affected users or as authorization to act.

**What equal actual spend means.** All three readers (shared-gap, keyword and fixed BM25) start with the same predeclared passage cap, independent of keyword availability. The private key retains all three native returns. If their actual charged inspections differ, rerun *each* at the smallest observed spend until they match. Each reduction strictly lowers the cap; zero is retained explicitly. Never pad a return, charge for a cache hit or grade an unmatched comparison as matched. These are separate offline runs: total elapsed work is not equal, nor does the matched result represent what an arm would have returned at its original cap.

Illustrative row shapes (repeat for every question/alias and add the actual commitments):
```json
{"interpreter":"reader1","worksheet_digest":"...","rows":[{"id":"q1","arm":"A","decision":"abstain","interpretation":"The record does not identify the recipient.","cites":[],"uncertainty":"Receipt is unverified.","unanswered":"Is a delivery record available?"}]}
```
```json
{"reviewer":"reviewer1","worksheet_digest":"...","answer_digest":"...","rows":[{"id":"q1","arm":"A","assessment":{"equivalent_evidence":"no","source_attribution":"not_applicable","unsupported_claim":"no","warranted_abstention":"yes"},"cites":[],"rationale":"The proposed action is documented but receipt is not.","unanswered":"Can the recipient confirm receipt?"}]}
```
Each dimension permits yes, no, uncertain or not_applicable. Spell out disagreement rather than converting these into a single score. Files are local operator artifacts; random aliases and digests do not authenticate reviewers or enforce external custody.

**Development rehearsal, not a held-out trial.** Reproduce the checked-in `reports/blind-inquiry-development.json` with `python scripts/rehearse_blind_inquiry.py DIRECTORY_WITH_BOTH_ATLAS_EXPORTS`. It verifies the exact published packet hashes before running. At the independent cap of four, matched spend was 15 inspections per method on GPT-4o (shared-gap 6/8; keyword and fixed BM25 7/8 each) and 14 on Astra (6/8 each). The native spend and every matched return are retained. Across both cases the methods therefore found 12, 13 and 13 designated passages at 29 inspections per method. No human answers or grades were fabricated for this exercise.
